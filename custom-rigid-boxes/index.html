Custom rigid boxes & magnetic gift boxes

Box styles we source

  • Two-piece rigid boxes — separate lid and base; sturdy and cost-effective.
  • Multi-pack boxes — 2-, 6- and 8-pack retail boxes with dividers.

Frequently asked questions

How much do custom rigid boxes cost?

For small-to-mid rigid boxes with a printed wrap, factory prices we see on real orders run roughly $0.49–$0.95 per box depending on size, closure and quantity (about 1,500 to 40,000+ boxes). Large boxes, specialty paper, foil and foam inserts cost more. Freight and duties are on top, so compare landed cost.

What is a magnetic box?

A magnetic box is a rigid box with a hinged lid that closes with hidden magnets. It is the classic premium unboxing box for gifts, cosmetics, electronics and accessories.

What is a two-piece rigid box?

A two-piece (lid and base) rigid box: a separate lid sits over a base. It is simple, sturdy and usually a little cheaper than a magnetic box.

What is the minimum order for rigid boxes?

Factory-direct rigid boxes usually make sense from about 1,500–3,000 boxes per design. The per-box price drops meaningfully at 10,000+.

Can you add foam or paper inserts?

Yes — EVA foam, sponge, paperboard and molded pulp inserts, cut to hold your product.

How do I avoid scuffed or damaged boxes?

Specify a scratch-resistant (anti-scuff) lamination, ask for corner protection and proper master cartons, and use a pre-shipment inspection. We build all of that into the order.
