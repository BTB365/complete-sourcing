Box styles we source

  • Magnetic closure boxes — hinged lid with hidden magnets; the premium gift-box standard.
  • Two-piece rigid boxes — separate lid and base; sturdy and cost-effective.
  • Multi-pack boxes — 2-, 6- and 8-pack retail boxes with dividers.
  • Folding cartons & printed retail boxes — lighter paperboard boxes for shelf and e-commerce.
  • Inserts — EVA foam, sponge, paperboard or molded pulp, cut to your product.
