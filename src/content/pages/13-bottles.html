Custom plastic bottles & jars

PET and HDPE bottles, jars and closures for supplements, cosmetics and personal care — stock shapes with your colors and decoration, or fully custom molds. Inspected and delivered duties-paid.

What we can source

  • Supplement bottles — packer bottles and wide-mouth jars in PET or HDPE.
  • Cosmetic & personal-care bottles — Boston rounds, cylinders, airless and pump bottles.
  • Jars — single- and double-wall jars for creams, balms and powders.
  • Closures — screw caps, child-resistant caps, flip-tops, pumps, sprayers and droppers.
  • Decoration — custom colors, silk-screen, hot stamping, labels and shrink sleeves.

Stock vs. custom: where the savings are

For most brands the best value is a stock bottle in a custom color with printed decoration. You skip mold costs and long lead times and still get a branded look. Custom molds make sense when shape is part of your brand or volumes are very high.

Pairs well with your boxes and clamshells

Because we already ship boxes and clamshells by the container, bottles can often ride in the same shipment — spreading freight across more goods and lowering your landed cost per piece.
