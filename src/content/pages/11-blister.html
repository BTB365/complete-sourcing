Blister packaging

Custom blister packaging & blister cards

Formed plastic blisters and printed blister cards for retail pegs and shelves — made by vetted factories, checked by third-party inspectors and delivered duties-paid to your warehouse.

Blister formats we source

  • Face-seal blister cards — the blister flange is heat-sealed to the front of a printed card. Lowest cost per unit at volume.
  • Full-face seal — the plastic covers the whole card face for a premium, tamper-resistant look.
  • Trapped blisters — the blister is held between two cards; no plastic-to-card sealing needed.
  • Double blisters — two formed halves around the product for 360° visibility.
  • Folding blisters — one-piece formed blisters that fold closed (overlapping with clamshells).

What drives blister packaging cost

  • Blister size and depth — more plastic and slower forming cycles.
  • Material and thickness — PET vs PVC vs PETG, 0.3 vs 0.6 mm.
  • Card printing — colors, coating (blister cards need a heat-seal coating), and card stock.
  • Mold — a one-time forming-mold cost, reused on reorders.
  • Freight and duties — blisters are light but bulky, so shipping per piece matters. We quote landed (DDP).
