Custom clamshell packaging, made to your product

Folding, trifold and hinged PET clamshells, formed to fit your product exactly — sourced direct from vetted thermoforming factories, inspected before shipment and delivered duties-paid to your U.S. warehouse.

Clamshells we source

  • Folding (hinged) clamshells — one-piece clamshells that fold closed around the product; the most common retail format.
  • Trifold clamshells — a third panel holds a printed insert card for branding and barcode, no heat-sealing required.
  • Double-blister / double-sided clamshells — two formed halves for products that need to be visible from both sides.
  • Hang-tab clamshells — with a euro-slot or round hole for peg display.
  • Embossed logo clamshells — your logo formed into the plastic; no printing cost per unit.

How a custom clamshell order works

  1. Spec & quote — send product dimensions (or a sample), quantity and target price.
  2. Drawing & mold — the factory produces a drawing; we check fit, wall strength and thickness.
  3. Physical samples — formed samples ship to you for fit testing (typically 1–3 rounds).
  4. Production — usually 2–4 weeks after sample approval.
  5. Pre-shipment inspection — third-party QC checks dimensions, clarity, cracking, counts and carton marks.
  6. DDP delivery — ocean freight, customs and duties handled; delivered to your 3PL.

Clamshell or blister card?

Clamshells need no heat-sealing equipment and are tougher to tamper with. Blister cards are cheaper per unit at very high volume and give you a full printed card. Not sure? Read clamshell vs. blister packaging.
