How we work

Your packaging sourcing agent, from factory to front door

We handle the parts that make factory-direct packaging risky — finding the right factory, samples, quality control, freight, customs and duties — and give you one landed price per piece.

1. Quote: real factory pricing, landed

Send your spec (or a sample), quantity and current price. We get pricing from factories that already make your type of packaging and quote you a landed, duties-paid price per piece — so you can compare apples to apples with your current supplier.

2. Factory selection & samples

We shortlist factories by capability, quality history and price, then manage drawings, tooling, artwork proofs and physical samples until you approve. Samples typically take 1–3 rounds.

3. Production with deposits that protect you

Orders typically run on a 30% deposit / 70% balance structure, with the balance paid only after the goods pass inspection.

4. Pre-shipment inspection

An independent third-party inspector checks every production run at the factory: dimensions, print and color, function (lids, magnets, hinges), defects, counts and carton marks. A typical inspection costs a few hundred dollars per visit — cheap insurance on a five- or six-figure order. How pre-shipment inspection works.

5. Freight, customs & duties (DDP)

We book ocean (full container or LCL) or air freight, clear U.S. customs, pay duties and tariffs, and schedule delivery to your 3PL or warehouse. Average ocean transit on our recent shipments was about 36 days from departure to arrival.

Who we work with

Product brands — often founder-run, often selling online — that buy the same packaging repeatedly and are paying a domestic supplier for it. If you order 10,000+ clamshells or a few thousand boxes at a time, we can almost certainly lower your cost.
