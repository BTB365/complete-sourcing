Why brands switch

Your domestic supplier is probably buying from the same factories.

A lot of the clamshells and boxes sold by U.S. packaging distributors are made overseas and resold with a markup. That markup is fine at a few hundred pieces. At 20,000, 50,000 or 100,000 units a year it adds up to real money.

The reason most brands don’t buy factory-direct isn’t price — it’s risk: finding a factory you can trust, getting samples right, checking quality, and dealing with freight, customs and tariffs. That is exactly the part we handle.
